IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

Understand what this platform can — and cannot — do.

Does Lexora AI provide legal advice?

No. The platform is designed to provide general legal information and help users organise their concerns. It does not replace advice from a qualified advocate or create an advocate-client relationship.

Can it predict whether I will win my case?

No. Legal outcomes depend on evidence, applicable law, procedure and judicial assessment. Lexora should not claim to predict verdicts, sentences, bail outcomes or case success.

Is my information private?

This frontend demo processes the chat locally in your browser and does not send it to a backend. A production service would need a clear privacy notice, secure processing, access controls, retention rules and appropriate consent.

Are the lawyer profiles real?

No. Profiles in this prototype are fictional examples used to demonstrate the interface. A live directory would require verification and a legally compliant operating model.

Which laws does Lexora cover?

This prototype does not contain a verified legal database. A production version should retrieve current legislation and judgments from authoritative sources and show citations with relevant dates.
